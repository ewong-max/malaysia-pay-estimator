PCB reliefs and method

  • Individual RM9,000; spouse with no income RM4,000; OKU RM7,000.
  • Child under 18 RM2,000; 18+ in degree studies RM8,000; disabled child RM8,000.
  • EPF relief capped at RM4,000; SOCSO + EIS at RM350.
  • RM400 rebate (plus RM400 for a non-working spouse) when chargeable income ≤ RM35,000.
  • Pay is assumed constant for 12 months, so PCB = yearly tax ÷ 12, rounded up to 5 sen. PCB under RM10 is not deducted. Zakat reduces PCB ringgit for ringgit.
  • Non-residents: flat 30% of monthly pay, no reliefs.

Employer cost

  • Gross salary for a promised net is solved backwards from the employee formula (no allowances or overtime).
  • Working days a year: 261 (5-day), 287 (5½-day), 313 (6-day). Daily rate = yearly salary ÷ working days.
  • Employment Act minimums: annual leave 8/12/16 days, sick leave 14/18/22 days by service; at least 11 paid public holidays.
  • HRD Corp levy charged on basic salary plus fixed allowances.
