# Gaji Bersih Malaysia

Single-page Malaysian take-home pay estimator (`index.html`).

- **Employee tab:** net pay after EPF, SOCSO, EIS and PCB (MTD), with a PCB breakdown.
- **Employer tab:** grosses up a promised net salary and adds employer EPF/SOCSO/EIS, HRD Corp levy, bonus, benefits and paid leave (annual, sick, public holidays).

Rates and assumptions are listed at the bottom of the page. Estimates only.
